Deck Football Rulebook

1. Deck Setup & Deal Quantities

  • Standard 52-card deck + 2 Jokers (54 cards total).
  • Fresh Drive Deal: Offense receives 8 cards; Defense receives 5 cards.
  • First Down Deal: Offense adds +7 cards; Defense adds +5 cards. (Cards accumulate across downs!).
  • Hand Cap: Maximum 12 cards held at any time. Any excess cards drawn beyond 12 must be discarded immediately.

2. Empty Hand Rule

  • If a player runs completely out of cards mid-drive, they must play subsequent downs with 0 cards (0 yards).
  • If Offense is empty, any card Defense plays becomes a loss / sack for that amount.
  • If Defense is empty, whatever Offense plays is unopposed yardage.
  • Punting Exception: If punting with an empty hand, draw 1 card from the deck. If a Joker is drawn, it is a muffed punt resulting in an immediate turnover right at the spot of the kick.

3. Card Values & Combos (v4 Rules)

  • Numbers (2–10): Face value (2 to 10 yards).
  • Face Cards (J, Q, K): Worth 10 yards each.
  • Defensive Aces: Permanently worth 11 yards.
  • Offensive Aces: Worth 11 yards if paired with a Queen; otherwise worth 5 yards.
  • Solo Jokers (Ace-Like): An unpaired Joker acts as an Ace for that player:
    • Defense: Worth 11 yards (can trigger the -10 yard sack cap).
    • Offense: Worth 11 yards if paired with a Queen; otherwise 5 yards.
  • Defensive Joker + King (Turnover): Causes an immediate Turnover UNLESS Offense also plays a King to protect the ball.
  • Offensive Joker + King (Penalty): Triggers an automatic +15 Yards & 1st Down penalty!

4. Tackle for Loss Clamping

  • Losses on standard downs are clamped at -5 Yards.
  • If the Defense plays an Ace or a Solo Joker (acting as an 11-point Ace), the sack cap extends down to -10 Yards.

5. Special Teams

  • On Field Goals and Punts, Aces are worth 11 yards for both teams.

6. Delay of Game

  • Offense has 30 seconds to call "HIKE" and lay down cards.
  • If the play clock reaches :00, a 5-yard penalty is enforced, game clock stops, and play clock resets to :30.

7. Safeties & Free Kicks

  • If a negative play pushes the ball backward into the offense's own endzone, the defense scores a Safety (+2 Points).
  • The team that conceded the safety must perform a Free Kick from their own 20-yard line using standard punt rules.

8. Halftime Discard & Reshuffle

  • Hands are maintained across all 1st downs and drives throughout each half.
  • Hands are only completely wiped clean and discarded at Halftime (end of Q2), reshuffling the entire 54-card deck for Q3.
