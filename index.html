Deck Football Official Rulebook

Complete v4 rules · Standard 52-card deck + 2 Jokers (54 cards)

1. Deck Setup & Deal Quantities

  • Deck: Standard 52-card playing deck plus 2 Jokers (54 cards total).
  • Fresh Drive Deal: At the start of every new possession (kickoff, punt, missed FG, or turnover), Offense receives 8 cards and Defense receives 5 cards.
  • First Down Deal: Whenever Offense earns a new 1st & 10, Offense adds +7 cards and Defense adds +5 cards. Unused cards stay in your hand and carry across downs and drives.
  • Hand Cap (12 Max): Neither player may hold more than 12 cards at any time. Any excess cards drawn beyond 12 must be discarded immediately.
  • Play Limit (3 Max): Each side may lay down between 0 and 3 cards per snap.

2. Opening Coin Flip, Kickoffs & Returns

  • Coin Flip: Before every new game, the Away team calls Heads or Tails on the digital coin flip. The winner chooses to Receive First or Defer to the 2nd half (the team that kicks off Q1 receives the Q3 kickoff).
  • Kickoff Options: On every kickoff (opening, 2nd half, post-TD, post-FG, and Overtime), the receiving team chooses either a Touchback (ball placed at Own 25) or a Kick Return.
  • Kick Return: Flip 4 physical cards from the deck and enter them into the companion app. The combined card total (Numbers = face value, Face = 10, Ace = 11) determines the return yardage from the goal line.

3. Empty Hand Rule

  • 0 Cards Held: If a player runs completely out of cards mid-drive, they must play subsequent downs with 0 cards (0 yards) until the next 1st down or change of possession.
  • Unopposed Plays: If Offense is empty, any cards Defense plays result in a loss/sack (subject to the TFL clamp). If Defense is empty, Offense gains unopposed yardage.
  • Punting Exception: If punting with an empty hand, draw 1 card from the deck. If a Joker is drawn, it is a muffed punt resulting in an immediate turnover right at the line of scrimmage.

4. v4 Card Values & Special Combos

  • Number Cards (2–10): Worth face value (2 to 10 yards).
  • Face Cards (J, Q, K): Worth 10 yards each.
  • Offensive Aces & Solo Jokers: Worth 11 yards when paired with a Queen (Q) on the same play; otherwise worth 1 yard.
  • Defensive Aces & Solo Jokers: Always worth 11 yards and unlock the extended -10 yard sack cap.
  • Offensive King + Joker (Penalty): Triggers an automatic +15-yard defensive penalty and automatic 1st Down (Pass Interference / Unnecessary Roughness).
  • Defensive King + Joker (Turnover): Causes an immediate Turnover at the spot unless Offense also plays a King on the same snap to protect the ball.

5. Tackle for Loss (TFL) Clamping

  • Standard Loss Cap: When Defense's card sum beats Offense's card sum on a standard play, negative yardage is clamped at -5 yards.
  • Ace / Joker Sack Cap: If Defense played an Ace or a Solo Joker on the play, the loss cap extends down to -10 yards.

6. 4th Down Decisions & Punting

  • 4th Down Prompt: Upon reaching 4th down, Offense chooses to Go For It, Punt, or attempt a Field Goal.
  • Punt Distance: Punting team plays 1 card: Numbers (2–10) = Value × 5 yards, Face cards (J/Q/K) = 45 yards, Ace = 50 yards.
  • Punt Return & Touchback: Receiving team may play 1 return card (Numbers = face value, Face = 10y, Ace = 11y) subtracted from the kick. Punts reaching the endzone result in a Touchback at the receiving team's 20-yard line.

7. Field Goals

  • Target Distance: Required yardage equals yards to opponent's goal line + 10. Net kick must strictly exceed the target (Net > Target) to be good for +3 points.
  • Offense Kick Cards: Draw 5 physical cards from the deck and optionally add up to 3 cards from hand (max 8 cards). Aces are worth 11 yards for both teams on Field Goals. Playing an Offense Joker adds a +25-yard kick boost.
  • Defense Contest: Defense may play up to 2 cards from hand to subtract from the kick. Playing a Defense Joker immediately blocks the kick.
  • Result: Good kicks award +3 and move to kickoff; missed or blocked kicks turn the ball over to Defense at the spot of the kick.

8. Touchdowns, PATs & Safeties

  • Touchdown (+6 Points): Crossing the opponent's goal line awards +6 points and proceeds through three phases: (1) choose 1-Pt Kick or 2-Pt Try, (2) resolve conversion result, and (3) kickoff to opponent.
  • 1-Point Extra Point: Flip the top card of the deck — 4 or higher is Good (+1); 2 or 3 misses.
  • 2-Point Conversion: Single-card snap (1 card each) — Offense must beat Defense for +2 points.
  • Safety (+2 Points): If a negative play pushes Offense into their own endzone, Defense scores +2 points and the conceding team must perform a Free Kick from their own 20-yard line using punt rules.

9. Clocks, Timeouts & Delay of Game

  • Quarter Length: Standard quarters are 3 minutes (configurable to 3, 5, or 7 minutes).
  • Synchronized Modal Pause: Whenever any calculator, decision, or deal modal opens, both the quarter clock and play clock automatically pause.
  • Timeouts: Use the single Timeout button to pause both clocks at any time; no timeouts are charged against either team.
  • 30-Second Play Clock: Offense has 30 seconds to call "HIKE" and lay down cards. Reaching :00 stops the game clock and triggers a 5-yard Delay of Game penalty (or wave-off).

10. Final 3 Seconds, Hail Mary, Halftime & Overtime

  • Final 3 Seconds Warning: With 0:03 left in either half (Q2 or Q4), Offense may attempt a buzzer-beater Field Goal, resume normal play, or launch a Hail Mary.
  • Hail Mary Bomb: Offense plays 3 cards (v4 Ace rules apply, total multiplied by ×2) against Defense's 2 cards. A Defense Joker is an endzone Interception; an Offense Joker is an automatic Miracle Touchdown; otherwise (Offense × 2) − Defense ≥ Yards to Endzone scores a Touchdown as the clock expires.
  • Halftime (End of Q2): Discard both players' hands completely and reshuffle all 54 cards before the Q3 kickoff.
  • Overtime: Played only if the score is tied at the end of Q4. Features a digital Coin Flip for first possession and a 2:00 Overtime clock.
