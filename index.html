Deck Football Rulebook

52-card deck + 2 Jokers (54 cards total)

1. Deck Setup & Hand Limits

  • Fresh Drive Deal: Offense receives 8 cards; Defense receives 5 cards.
  • First Down Deal: Offense adds +7 cards; Defense adds +5 cards. Unused cards carry over.
  • Hand Cap: Maximum 12 cards held at any time. Discard any excess immediately.
  • Play Limit: Up to 3 cards played per side on each snap.

2. Card Values & Joker Mechanics

  • Numbers (2–10): Worth face value in yards.
  • Face Cards (J, Q, K): Worth 10 yards each.
  • Aces: Automatically worth 11 yards (optional v4 mode sets Offense Ace to 11y with Queen, else 5y).
  • Offensive Joker Penalty (or K + Joker): +15-yard penalty and automatic 1st Down (+25 yards on Field Goal attempts).
  • Defensive Joker + King: Immediate Turnover unless Offense also plays a King to protect the ball.
  • Tackle for Loss Cap: Standard losses clamp at -5 yards; Defensive Ace or solo Joker extends the sack cap to -10 yards.

3. Empty Hand Rule

  • If a side runs out of cards mid-drive, they play subsequent downs with 0 cards (0 yards).
  • Punting Exception: Draw 1 card from the deck. Drawing a Joker is a muffed punt and immediate turnover at the spot.

4. Special Teams, Clocks & Halftime

  • Punts: Card value × 5 yards (Face = 45y, Ace = 50y) minus Defense return card. Landing in the endzone is a touchback to the 20.
  • Field Goals: Offense draws 5 deck cards + up to 3 hand cards (Joker = +25y boost) vs. up to 2 defense cards (Joker = Block). Net must strictly exceed distance to goal + 10.
  • Delay of Game: 30-second play clock. Reaching :00 triggers a 5-yard penalty.
  • Safeties: Tackled in own endzone awards +2 to Defense and triggers a Free Kick from the 20.
  • Halftime (End of Q2): Discard both hands and reshuffle the entire 54-card deck for Q3.
